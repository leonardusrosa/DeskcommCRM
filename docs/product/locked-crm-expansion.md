# Locked CRM expansion

## Google Calendar + native booking
Deskcomm owns scheduling UX/rules; Google Calendar is the primary calendar engine/sync layer. No Cal.com/Calendly dependency in v1 unless a concrete blocker is proven.

## Google Review Lite
Native review-request workflow using a configured Google review URL and existing WhatsApp delivery. No Google Business Profile API dependency in v1.

## CRM AI Copilot
Operator-facing copilot grounded in contact, conversation, opportunity and CRM memory/context. V1 is read/draft/suggest; customer-facing writes require explicit operator confirmation.

## Excluded
- Omnichannel expansion
- Landing-page builder
